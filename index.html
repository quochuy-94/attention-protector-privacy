Privacy Policy — Attention Protector

Package: com.attentionprotector.app • Effective: November 18, 2026 • Last Updated: November 19, 2026

1. Core Privacy Guarantee: Zero Network Access

Attention Protector is built so your attention habits, app usage, and personal communications never leave your phone.

  • No INTERNET Permission: The app does not request android.permission.INTERNET or android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE. Android OS physically blocks any outbound or inbound network traffic.
  • Zero Analytics, Crash Telemetry, or Ads: Contains zero tracking SDKs, cloud backends, accounts, or ad networks.
  • Cloud Backup Disabled: Local statistics stay on-device only (android:allowBackup="false").

2. Android AccessibilityService Disclosure

Android provides no standard API to distinguish specific feeds inside another app (such as Instagram Reels vs. Direct Messages, or YouTube Shorts vs. educational videos). When enabled, Attention Protector uses AccessibilityService (android:isAccessibilityTool="false") strictly on-device.

  • OS-Level Package Filtering: Events are scoped exclusively to the guarded entertainment, shopping, news, or custom apps you turn on via serviceInfo.packageNames. Banking apps, keyboards, launchers, and unguarded apps never deliver events.
  • What Is Read Ephemerally in RAM: Public UI view IDs, class names, selection states, short descriptions, and layout bounds strictly to detect guarded feeds or draw a non-touchable visual mask (TYPE_ACCESSIBILITY_OVERLAY) over inline Home-feed Reels when enabled.
  • What Is Never Read or Saved: Keystrokes, passwords, PINs, personal chat content, posts, screenshots, or financial credentials are never read, recorded, or transmitted.
All accessibility tree inspection happens transiently in volatile memory and is discarded immediately.

3. Optional Notification Listener Access

If you choose to enable the optional Unreplied Chat Shortcuts feature, Attention Protector observes active notification sender names and reply intents from personal messaging apps (WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram DMs, Zalo, SMS) solely in RAM to let you jump back into pending conversations.

  • Banking alerts, OTP codes, and system alerts are filtered out immediately.
  • No notification message body is stored on disk or transmitted. Core blocking works 100% without this permission.

4. Local Data Storage & Deletion Controls

Only local summary counts, outcomes (RESISTED, REDIRECTED, PUSHED_THROUGH), and your preferences are stored in private on-device storage.

  • Instant In-App Purge: Tap Settings → Emergency & Data Controls → Clear Activity History to delete all local activity records immediately.
  • OS Reset / Uninstall: Clearing app storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app permanently removes all data.

5. Children's Privacy & Contact

Attention Protector is intended for individuals aged 13 and older and does not knowingly market to or collect data from children under 13.
